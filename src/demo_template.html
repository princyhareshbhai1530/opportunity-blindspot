    How it works

    1. Clone. Copy one candidate profile into two twins.
    2. Vary history only. Twin A browsed senior roles; Twin B browsed entry-level roles.
    3. Rank. Same ranker, same pool of the jobs that best match the profile.
    4. Compare. Check each feed against the twin's own profile-only Top .
    5. Score. Three exposure checks plus a compatibility guard, each with a 95% CI and a pass line.
    Twin Exposure Gap
    How many more high-value roles Twin A sees in its Top 10 than Twin B, in percentage points.
    Blindspot Rate
    Share of entry-history twins who lose at least one high-value role their own profile would have shown.
    Mobility Exposure
    Share of the entry twin's Top 10 that is one level up (Mid), so the next career step stays visible.
    Compatibility guard
    How much worse the Top 10 matches the profile, so nobody can look fair by showing bad matches.

    Method and limits

    • Data. JTH v1.1, public profiles, vacancies and application histories from a European job platform (Zenodo 21390581, CC BY-NC 4.0).
    • Split. Candidates split 50/50 by ID into a tune half and a test half. Every number here is from the test half.
    • Seed. for all sampling; bootstrap resamples for every CI.
    • Pre-registration. Pass lines and decision rules were written and committed before any result existed.
    • Limits. A controlled simulation, not an audit of a real platform. European data, not Canadian. Twin histories are synthetic. Compatibility is a text-similarity proxy, not verified eligibility. We use no demographic fields and make no claim about any group.
